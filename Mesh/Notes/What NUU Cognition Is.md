# What NUU Cognition Is

NUU Cognition is an organisation that advances the domain of thinking technologies. A thinking technology is a tool that helps a mind build, share, and use models of reality. See [[Thinking Technologies]].

NUU Cognition is not one product. It is the connective tissue for a whole domain. The core bet is this: thinking is a complex system. It spans learning, organising, reasoning, communicating, deciding, and acting. No single app covers all of that. The value comes from the compound effect between tools, research, and shared standards.

## The four goals

The organisation states four goals. They are recursive: each one supports the others.

1. **Advance cognitive capability.** Increase what a human mind can process, hold, and resolve into understanding.
2. **Engineer the infrastructure of thought.** Build the protocols, data structures, and interfaces that let concepts flow between minds and machines.
3. **Universal scale.** Make these tools available to the general population, not to a small elite.
4. **The entity as a system.** Run the organisation with its own tools. NUU Cognition is the first test subject of its own thesis.

As of November 2025, the organisation takes the form of a technology startup. The form is secondary to the mission.

## What it builds

NUU Cognition starts with software. The products form one stack:

- **Flint** is a workspace for a person and an AI agent. See [[What a Flint Is]].
- **Shards** are cognitive programs that extend what an agent can do inside a Flint. See [[Shards]].
- **Mesh** is the data structure under everything: markdown files and media that link to each other. See [[The Mesh]].
- **NUU Cognition Markdown (NCM)** is a flavour of markdown that treats a document as a series of blocks.
- **Vessel** publishes a Mesh to the web.
- **Surf** is a zoomable reading interface.
- **Parse** and **NUU Docs** bring external documents into the ecosystem.

See [[The Other Products]] for one paragraph on each product.

## How the parts connect

Flint is a Mesh editor. Vessel is a Mesh publisher. NCM is how both parse and render markdown. Mesh and NCM are the core data primitives. They are designed to be friendly to humans, to language models, and to machines.

The organisation uses every part of the stack in its own daily work. Its public pitch was written inside a Flint and published through Vessel. The feedback loop is short because the builders are the core users.

## The vision in plain terms

You read a document. A tool breaks it into ideas and shows their connections. You comment, share, and add ideas to your own knowledge base. An agent helps you research and trace ideas to their origins. A tool helps you write, and then helps you publish in the form that fits. That is one end-to-end workflow, from discovery to output. Individual companies solve individual pieces. NUU Cognition works on the whole chain.
